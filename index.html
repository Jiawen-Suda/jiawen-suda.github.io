First Author

  1. 2026

    Mechanistic Origins of Enhanced PET Hydrolase Activity from Molecular Dynamics and Deep Learning-Assisted Network Analysis

    Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling (2026).

    DOI: 10.1021/acs.jcim.6c01736 ↗

    Developing efficient PET-degrading enzymes is important for plastic recycling. This study combines molecular dynamics simulations, enhanced sampling, and deep learning models to investigate the mechanisms underlying enhanced PET hydrolase activity at the pre-reaction stage, providing mechanistic insights into the complex, multiphase process of enzymatic PET hydrolysis.

  2. 2025

    Enhancing the understandings on SARS-CoV-2 main protease (Mpro) mutants from molecular dynamics and machine learning

    International Journal of Biological Macromolecules, 310, 143076 (2025).

    DOI: 10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2025.143076 ↗

    The COVID-19 pandemic has posed major challenges to global public health, with viral evolution and drug resistance underscoring the need for continued optimization of antiviral therapies. This study develops a computational framework integrating molecular dynamics simulations, free energy perturbation, AlphaFold 3, and deep learning models to systematically investigate the molecular interactions and binding thermodynamics of representative SARS-CoV-2 main protease mutants with nirmatrelvir and substrates, providing a theoretical basis for understanding the molecular mechanisms of resistance mutations and designing next-generation inhibitors.
